Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2025 | Compliant with Shopify App Store, GDPR & CPRA
Zero Personal Data (PII) Architecture
Core Privacy Commitment: PulseMascot is designed with a strict zero-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) privacy-by-design architecture. We never collect, track, store, or sell shopper names, email addresses, phone numbers, shipping addresses, IP addresses, or payment credentials.

1. Information We Collect

PulseMascot collects only the minimal data strictly required to deliver gamified cart milestone rewards and conversion rate optimization (CRO) analytics for Shopify merchants:

  • Merchant Store Configuration: Store domain (e.g., store.myshopify.com), selected mascot persona, unlocked tier thresholds, reward labels, and exit intent discount codes.
  • Aggregated Anonymous Storefront Telemetry: Anonymized numeric counters such as mascot impressions, click interactions, milestone unlock counts, and total cart value progression calculated live in the shopper's browser via Shopify's standard /cart.js client-side API.

2. How Data is Stored, Processed & Protected

All merchant configuration and aggregate conversion metrics are stored in secured, encrypted environments with strict access controls. Telemetry transmissions use TLS/HTTPS encryption. At no point is shopper browsing history linked to an individual identity.

3. GDPR, UK-GDPR & CPRA Compliance

We comply fully with European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK-GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CPRA). Because we do not store customer PII, shopper records are inherently protected against unauthorized disclosure.

We implement and support all mandatory Shopify Privacy Webhook endpoints:

  • customers/data_request: Inquiries regarding stored customer data return confirmation that zero customer PII is retained.
  • customers/redact: Immediate confirmation that no customer PII exists within our systems.
  • shop/redact: Automated purge of all store configuration and aggregate metrics within 48 hours of an uninstallation.
  • app/uninstalled: Immediate cessation of all data sync operations and session revocation upon app removal.

4. Data Retention Timelines

Aggregate store analytics are retained solely for the duration of the merchant's active app installation. If a merchant uninstalls PulseMascot, all stored configuration and metrics are automatically and permanently erased after 48 hours following the shop/redact webhook trigger.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

PulseMascot utilizes minimal client-side browser storage (sessionStorage or localStorage) strictly to prevent repetitive sound triggers or duplicate exit-intent popups within a single shopping session. These cookies/tokens contain no tracking identifiers and are never shared across websites.
